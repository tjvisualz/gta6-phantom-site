GTA 6 Phantom

GTA 6 Phantom is a personal desktop app run by Teejay Visualz. It gathers public GTA 6 and Rockstar Games news, makes news cards and clips from my own recordings, and posts them to my own GTA 6 Phantom accounts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube.

It is not a public service: only the owner signs in, and only to his own accounts.
