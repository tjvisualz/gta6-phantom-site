Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

GTA 6 Phantom is a private desktop app used only by its owner, Teejay Visualz, on his own computer.

What it stores

When the owner signs in to his own Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X or YouTube accounts, the app keeps the sign-in tokens and basic account details (name, profile picture, follower count) on that computer only, encrypted. It stores no data about other people.

What it shares

Nothing is sold or shared. Data is only sent to the platform it belongs to, in order to post the owner's content.

Removing data

Signing out in the app, or removing the app's access in the platform's settings, deletes or revokes the stored sign-in.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/tjvisualz/gta6-phantom-site.
